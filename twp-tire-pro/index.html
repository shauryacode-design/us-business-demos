GOOD TO KNOW

BEFORE YOU
HIT THE ROAD.

Have a question about tires or our services? Here are a few helpful answers.

☎ Call the Shop

Check the sidewall of your current tire. You'll see a code such as 225/65R17. You can also check your vehicle owner's manual or the tire information label on your vehicle.

Some punctures can be repaired depending on their location and the tire's condition. Contact us so the shop can assess your situation and advise on timing.

Same-day service depends on the service required, tire availability, and shop workload. Call us to check current availability.

Contact the shop to confirm walk-in availability or ask about arranging a visit. If the shop has an existing booking tool, use the booking link provided by the business.

Warranty coverage depends on the tire brand and the terms supplied by the shop or manufacturer. Ask us for the applicable details before purchasing.

Visible damage, unusual wear, cracks, bulges, or low tread can indicate a tire needs professional attention. Have your tires inspected if you are unsure.
